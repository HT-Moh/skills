# Format catalog, timeboxes, and how to choose

Reference for Step 2 of the retrospective skill. Read when picking a format beyond the
short table in SKILL.md, when planning timeboxes for a live meeting, or when the team
shows retro fatigue.

## The meeting skeleton: Derby & Larsen's five phases

Every format below is an *exercise* that slots into phases 2–4 of this skeleton
(*Agile Retrospectives: Making Good Teams Great*, Derby & Larsen 2006; 2nd ed. 2024):

| Phase | Purpose | 60-min split |
|---|---|---|
| 1. Set the stage | Transition into reflection; every voice heard once early — someone silent here likely stays silent | 10 |
| 2. Gather data | Shared picture: objective numbers + subjective experience | 10 |
| 3. Generate insights | Root causes — teams that skip this jump to conclusions | 20 |
| 4. Decide what to do | 1–3 owned actions; without this phase it's a complaint session | 15 |
| 5. Close | Retro-on-the-retro, appreciation | 5 |

The recurring failure: Gather Data expands to fill time and squeezes Insights + Decide —
exactly backwards, since those two phases determine whether anything changes. Start
prioritizing about halfway through, whatever the total length.

## Timeboxing

- Rule of thumb: **30–45 min of retro per week of iteration**. Scrum Guide ceiling:
  3 hours for a one-month sprint.
- 1-week sprint → 30–45 min · 2-week → 60–90 min · 4-week → ~120 min.
- **30-min compression**: Set stage 5 / Gather+Insights merged 10 (silent writing, then
  cluster) / Decide 10 / Close 5. Keep the check-in even at 5 min; protect Decide.
- Remote sync meetings: cap at ~50 min; attention degrades hard after that.

## Catalog

| Format | Mechanics | Best fit | Length |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Start / Stop / Continue** | Three columns of behaviors; output is already action-shaped | New teams, first retros, routine sprints, tight slots. Goes stale fast; no emotional channel | 30–45 |
| **Plus / Delta** | Working (+) vs change-needed (Δ); "change" framing avoids negativity | Fastest option; micro-retros at meeting end | 20–30 |
| **Mad / Sad / Glad** | Three emotional buckets; process feelings before solutions | Decompression after a rough, high-stress period. Needs safety; doesn't naturally yield actions — bolt Step 4 on firmly | 30–45 |
| **4Ls** — Liked / Learned / Lacked / Longed for | Balances enjoyment, knowledge, gaps, aspirations | Milestones, releases, project ends; also the classic *personal* retro format | 45–60 |
| **Sailboat** | Wind (propels), anchors (drags), rocks (risks), island (goal) on a drawn boat | Breaking feedback stagnation; visual thinkers; scales to 15+ people; shifts gaze forward | 45–60 |
| **Starfish** — keep / less / more / stop / start | "Less/more" wedges capture dosage nuance binary formats miss | Experienced teams calibrating rather than overhauling | 45–60 |
| **KALM** — keep / add / less / more | Starfish minus the hard stop/start; low-pressure framing | Fine-tuning a workflow that mostly works | 45–60 |
| **DAKI** — drop / add / keep / improve | Separates "abandon" from "refine" | Mature teams making decisive process change | 45–60 |
| **Timeline** | Chronological wall of events, color-coded by emotion; then mine for patterns and inflection points | Long spans: 4-week sprints, releases, quarters, project retros | 60–90 |
| **Lean Coffee** | No preset agenda: propose topics, dot-vote, discuss in strict priority order in 5–8 min timeboxes | Many burning topics; self-directed mature groups; large groups. Disadvantages quiet members | 45–60 |
| **Three Little Pigs** | Straw (fragile) / sticks (stable-but) / bricks (solid); start with bricks to prime positives | Risk review of newly shipped work; makes tech-debt discussable playfully | 45–60 |
| **Hopes & Fears** | Aspirations vs concerns about what's *coming* (a futurespective) | Kickoffs, new teams, org change. Not for reviewing a past iteration | 30–45 |

## Choosing and rotating

- **Rotate every 2–4 sprints**, not every sprint — a format needs a few runs to get
  fluent, but the same one forever produces autopilot answers. Say *why* you switched.
- **Event overrides rotation**: an incident, a release, a team change picks the format
  regardless of whose turn it is.
- By experience: new team → Start/Stop/Continue · some experience → 4Ls, Mad/Sad/Glad,
  Sailboat · seasoned → DAKI, Starfish, Lean Coffee.
- By mood: disengaged → visual metaphor (concrete imagery gives quiet members a prompt
  instead of a blank canvas) · low morale → positives-first · tense → emotion-first plus
  anonymous input.
- Whatever the format: it must end in commitments. A format is a data engine, not the
  meeting.
